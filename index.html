Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need to do anything?

Almost nothing. We build it from information that's already public about your business, then check the details with you. Most owners spend about ten minutes on the whole thing.

What if I already have a website?

Then you may not need us. If yours is outdated or doesn't work properly on a phone, we're happy to show you what a replacement would look like — no obligation either way.

Do I own the website?

Yes. It's your business, your domain, and your content. If you cancel, you can take the site with you.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month. If it isn't earning its keep, cancel and we part on good terms.

How long does it take?

Usually two to three days from the moment you say go. Rush jobs can be faster.

Will this put me at the top of Google?

Honestly, not on its own and not overnight. A good website is what makes ranking possible — without one you can't compete at all. We build it properly so the work you do afterwards actually counts.
